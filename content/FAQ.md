::dropdown{title="What are the rules?"}
- The following sections will explain the general ruling for each game mode.

::::dropdown{title="Hide & Seek"}
- Before the round starts a :s-icon{name="seeker"}**Seeker** will be chosen (usually by spinning a [**wheel**](https://wheelofnames.com/)). Everyone will head to the odyssey and the :s-icon{name="seeker"}**Seeker** will start a one-minute countdown. 
During that minute all :s-icon{name="hider"}**Hiders** will go to their hiding spots. Once the countdown ends, the :s-icon{name="seeker"}**Seeker** may begin to search. Players are given a one minute grace period after the :s-icon{name="seeker"}**Seeker** start searching. 
If a :s-icon{name="hider"}**Hider** dies while reaching their hiding spot during the grace period, they may return to that same spot. This does not apply if they are getting chased by a :s-icon{name="seeker"}**Seeker**. After the grace period ends, any death counts as being caught.

## Asking for Hints
- Every 3 minutes or so, you must give a hint when the :s-icon{name="seeker"}**Seeker(s)** request. They can ask simple hints at first, like: 
- **Sub or Overworld**: Whether or not you are hiding in a subarea, or the kingdom overworld
- **Top or Bottom**: Whether you are in the bottom half or top half when you press :s-icon{name="minus"}
- **Left or Right**: Whether you are in the Left half or Right half of the map when you press :s-icon{name="minus"}
- 
- After a certain amount of time where the :s-icon{name="hider"}**Hider(s)** are not found the :s-icon{name="seeker"}**Seeker(s)** can ask for more direct hints 
which include cords from the in-game map or sending a cutout of a screenshot. 
- **Row** (Letter): C
- **Column** (Number): 2
- **Cell** (Both): C2

## Places that are forbidden to hide in
-   Other kingdoms.
-   Inside the Odyssey.
-   Inside captures (using static captures like power lines temporarily to move somewhere is fine).
-   Inside something without collision that occludes the player completely (e.g. trees).
-   At a position where an object was located at before interacting with it (e.g. crates or chests).
-   In areas inaccessible by others (e.g. rooms only accessible by wearing specific costumes).
-   In special areas (e.g. the boss fight paintings in Mushroom Kingdom or race events like Koopa Freerunning).
-   Out of bounds.
::::

::::dropdown{title="Sardines"}
- It is very similar to Hide & Seek but it works differently. The :s-icon{name="sardines_group"}Sardines Pack
(one player) hides while everyone else :s-icon{name="sardines_solo"}Single Sardine searches & when a player finds them, they join them and it will eventually start to form a larger group. The last player to find the group ends that round.

- Same rules when asking for hints.
::::

::::dropdown{title="Freeze Tag"}
- Freeze Tag is more skill-based as players are split into :s-icon{name="chaser"}**Chasers** & :s-icon{name="runner"}**Runners**. As the host can choose teams via manually or randomize them (usually the [wheel](https://wheelofnames.com/)). :s-icon{name="chaser"}**Chasers** have an arrow above their head so they know where a nearby :s-icon{name="runner"}**Runner(s)** is.
- If all :s-icon{name="runner"}**Runners** are caught, the :s-icon{name="chaser"}**Chasers** score a wipeout bonus that is 20 points each.


### Places that are forbidden to go
- Entering sub-areas.
- Using captures for any advantages (using static captures like power lines temporarily to move somewhere is fine).

### Note for Runners
- Survive as long as possible.
- Save your frozen teammates via touching them and you can find them from their name tags
- Use movement skills to avoid being tagged.
- If a player falls into a death plane, they are bubbled back to their last safe position.
::::

::dropdown{title="Do emulators work with this mod?"}
Anything that can play Switch games *should* work with this mod, but make sure you are __not__ using the default profile if you use an emulator. Our [compatibility](/compatibility) page should list each one.
::


::dropdown{title="How do I switch gamemodes?"}
Go to the SMOO menu — see the **controls** section — and there should be options for some gamemode tweaks.

:::alert{type="info"}
**Note:** base SMOO does *not* have any gamemodes. It can only enable custom gravity.
:::
::


::dropdown{title="How can I fix my game crashing?"}
Make sure that your using the builds provided from the [Play](/Play) section alternatively you should use the Sardines build as it the most stable then SMOO+ & Crafty's offical build.

:::alert{type="warning"}
Be sure you ***replace SMOO with the new version***. It may not prompt you for a new IP address on launch.
:::

::dropdown{title="My friend and I can't see each other!"}
- Make sure the [server is online](https://smoo.it/#/servers).
- Check your debug menu ( :s-icon{name="zl"} + :s-icon{name="up"} while unpaused) for the `Socket Connection Status: Socket Connected` line.
- Get everyone to warp at least once.
- If you are on emulator, make sure the profile you are using:
    - Has less than 10 characters in its name.
    - Is *not* the default profile and does not have the default profile name.

For more in-depth solutions, take a look at [these quick fixes from the Discord](https://discord.com/channels/481991664085499924/481993523424395297/1149492186254163968).

::dropdown{title="I have the mod installed and I can see the SMOO icon but was never asked for an IP address"}

Most likely:

- Your **game** is not on `1.0.0`.
    - SMOO only works on `1.0.0` and it's highly likely it will stay that way. Modding for SMO `1.3.0`+ is more convoluted and thus not realistic for a mod of this complexity.
- You didn't install the mod correctly.
    - Make sure the mod folder has the following two folders in it: `romfs` and `exefs`. Also make sure those files have contents in them.
